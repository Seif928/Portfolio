## Architecture & Engineering Highlights

- **Zero-Build Editorial Frontend:** Semantic `index.html`, token-driven `styles.css`, data-driven hydration in `app.js` + `portfolio-data.js`, and progressive motion in `motion.js`.
- **Interactive Device Galleries & Full-Screen Lightbox:** 57 verified project slides across 6 real projects with composite iPhone/Android frames, touch swipe gestures, keyboard navigation, focus trapping, `inert` background management, and progressive WebP loading with automatic PNG fallback.
- **Performance & Accessibility:** Instant Hero LCP rendering, responsive breakpoint symmetry (`320px` to `1920px`), `prefers-reduced-motion` support, and WCAG AA contrast and touch targets (`44×44 px`).
